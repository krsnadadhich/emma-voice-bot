Deliverail - Delivery Options

Standard delivery arrives in 2 to 3 working days.

Next-day delivery arrives the next working day for parcels handed over by the
4:00pm cut-off, Monday to Friday. Parcels handed over after 4:00pm travel the
following day.

Same-day delivery is available in selected city zones only. It must be booked
before 11:00am and is delivered by 8:00pm the same day.

Saturday delivery is available on request for an extra charge of 4.50 pounds.

Every delivery gets a two-hour delivery window, sent by text message on the
morning of delivery. A signature is required for parcels worth over 100 pounds.
You can ask for a delivery slot on a specific day when you book, and we will
offer the nearest available windows.
