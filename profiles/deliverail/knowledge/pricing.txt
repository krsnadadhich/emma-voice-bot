Deliverail - Pricing

Prices are per parcel and depend on weight and speed. All prices include VAT.

Up to 2 kilograms: standard 4.99 pounds, next-day 7.99 pounds.
2 to 10 kilograms: standard 7.99 pounds, next-day 11.99 pounds.
10 to 25 kilograms: standard 12.99 pounds, next-day 18.99 pounds.

Same-day delivery starts from 14.99 pounds depending on the city zone.

Parcels over 25 kilograms are treated as freight and need a quote from the
customer team. Every parcel includes protection up to 50 pounds. Extra cover
costs 1 pound for every 100 pounds of value.

There is no charge for dropping a parcel at one of our depots. A doorstep
pickup is a flat 2 pounds on top of the delivery price.
