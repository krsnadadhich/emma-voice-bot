Deliverail - Tracking an Order

Every order has a six-digit order number, printed on the parcel label and
included in the confirmation email. Give that number to this phone line and
we will read out the current status and the expected delivery window.

What each status means:
- Label created: the order is registered and waiting to be collected.
- Picked up: the parcel has been collected and is heading to our depot.
- At depot: the parcel is at a Deliverail depot and is being sorted.
- Out for delivery: the parcel is on the driver's van and will arrive today.
- Delivered: the parcel has been handed over or left in the chosen safe place.
- Delivery failed: the driver could not hand over the parcel.
- Delayed: the parcel is running late, for example because of weather.

Status updates are refreshed every few hours. The two-hour delivery window is
confirmed by text message on the morning of delivery.
