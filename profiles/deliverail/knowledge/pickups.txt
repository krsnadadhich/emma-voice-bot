Deliverail - Booking a Pickup

A driver can collect a parcel from your address, Monday to Saturday. Book the
pickup at least 24 hours ahead through this phone line and pick a time slot.

The parcel must be packed, sealed and labelled before the driver arrives.
Drivers cannot pack items for you. A single parcel can weigh up to 25
kilograms for a doorstep pickup.

Doorstep pickup costs a flat 2 pounds. Dropping a parcel at the Central depot
or the East depot is free and needs no booking.

You can cancel or move a pickup up to 2 hours before the slot at no charge.
