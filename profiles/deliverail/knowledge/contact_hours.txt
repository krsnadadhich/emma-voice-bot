Deliverail - Customer Team Hours and Depots

The Deliverail customer team is available Monday to Friday, 8:00am to 7:00pm,
and Saturday, 9:00am to 1:00pm. We are closed on Sundays and bank holidays.

Calls are answered fastest between 10:00am and noon. The busiest time is
Monday morning, so a call later in the week is usually quicker.

Deliverail runs two public depots for drop-offs and collections:
- Central depot, open Monday to Saturday, 8:00am to 6:00pm.
- East depot, open Monday to Friday, 9:00am to 5:00pm.

Outside customer team hours you can still track an order using its six-digit
order number, and book a delivery or pickup slot through this phone line.
