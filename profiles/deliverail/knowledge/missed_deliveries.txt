Deliverail - Missed Deliveries and Redelivery

If nobody is available, the driver leaves a card and the parcel returns to the
depot. The first redelivery is free and takes place the next working day.

You can choose a redelivery day, ask for the parcel to be left in a safe place,
or nominate a neighbour to receive it. Book a new delivery slot through this
phone line or by replying to the text message.

After a second failed attempt the parcel is held at the depot for 7 days and
then returned to the sender. To collect a held parcel, bring the order number
and photo identification to the depot.
