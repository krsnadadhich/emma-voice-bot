Deliverail - Returns, Damage and Lost Parcels

To send a parcel back, the sender requests a return label within 14 days of
delivery. Return parcels are priced the same as an outbound parcel.

If a parcel arrives damaged, report it within 48 hours of delivery and keep the
packaging and the damaged item for inspection.

A parcel is treated as lost after 10 working days with no tracking movement.
Report it to the customer team and a claim is opened straight away.

Claims are paid up to the protected value of the parcel, which is 50 pounds
unless extra cover was bought. Approved claims are paid within 5 to 7 working
days.
