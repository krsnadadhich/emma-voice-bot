Riverside Green Surgery — Getting Your Test Results

Most blood test and screening results are available within 3-5 working
days, though some specialist tests can take up to 2 weeks.

You can check results in three ways:
1. Through the NHS App, if you're registered for online services — most
   routine results appear here automatically once reviewed by a clinician.
2. By calling the surgery after 2:00pm, when results lines are less busy.
3. By waiting for the surgery to contact you, which only happens if a
   result needs discussion or requires action.

Results are not given out over the phone by reception staff — only by a
clinician, and only once a GP has reviewed and signed off the result.

If a test result is urgent or abnormal, the surgery will contact you
directly rather than waiting for you to call in.
