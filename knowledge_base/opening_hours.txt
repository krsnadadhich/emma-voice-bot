Riverside Green Surgery — Opening Hours

The surgery is open Monday to Friday, 8:00am to 6:30pm.
We are closed on Saturdays, Sundays, and all public/bank holidays.

Phone lines open at 8:00am for same-day appointment requests. The lines are
often busiest between 8:00am and 9:30am, so calling slightly later in the
morning may mean a shorter wait.

The building's front doors are locked from 6:30pm. If you have a booked
appointment running late in the day, use the intercom at the main entrance.

For anything outside these hours, see the out-of-hours and emergency
guidance document.
