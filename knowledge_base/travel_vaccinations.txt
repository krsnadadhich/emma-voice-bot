Riverside Green Surgery — Travel Vaccinations

Some travel vaccinations are available free on the NHS, including typhoid,
hepatitis A, and cholera, depending on your destination.

Other travel vaccinations, such as yellow fever, Japanese encephalitis,
and rabies, are not available on the NHS and must be booked as a private
travel clinic appointment, which carries a charge.

Please book a travel health consultation at least 6-8 weeks before you
travel, as some vaccination courses require multiple doses spaced weeks
apart. Late bookings may mean full protection isn't achieved before travel.

Bring your travel itinerary (countries, regions, and dates) to the
appointment so the nurse can advise on exactly what you need.
