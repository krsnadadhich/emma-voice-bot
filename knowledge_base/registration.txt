Riverside Green Surgery — New Patient Registration

We accept new patients who live within our practice boundary, which covers
the RG14 to RG18 postcode areas. You can check whether your address is
inside the boundary by asking reception or checking the practice website.

To register, you need to:
1. Complete a GMS1 registration form, available at reception or online.
2. Provide proof of address (a recent utility bill or bank statement).
3. Provide photo ID if available (passport or driving licence), though this
   is not strictly required to register.

You do not need a fixed address, immigration status, or NHS number to
register — everyone is entitled to register with a GP surgery.

Registration usually takes 2-3 working days to process. You will be
contacted to book a new patient health check once registration is complete.
