Riverside Green Surgery — Out-of-Hours and Urgent Care

Outside of surgery opening hours (before 8:00am, after 6:30pm, weekends,
and bank holidays), routine appointments are not available.

For urgent medical concerns that cannot wait until the surgery reopens,
call NHS 111, available 24 hours a day, for advice and, if needed,
onward referral to an out-of-hours GP service or urgent treatment centre.

For life-threatening emergencies — such as chest pain, difficulty
breathing, severe bleeding, loss of consciousness, or suspected stroke —
call 999 or go directly to your nearest A&E department immediately. Do
not wait for a callback from the surgery or NHS 111 in these situations.

The nearest A&E department is Riverside General Hospital, roughly 15
minutes from the surgery by car.
