Riverside Green Surgery — Fit Notes (Sick Notes)

For the first 7 calendar days of an illness, you do not need a fit note
from a GP — you can self-certify using an SC2 form for your employer.

If you are unwell for longer than 7 days, you may need a fit note from a
clinician. This is usually issued following a telephone or in-person
consultation, not simply on request.

Fit notes are not issued retrospectively for time already taken off
without a prior consultation, except in unusual circumstances at the
clinician's discretion.

To arrange a fit note consultation, book a routine appointment via
reception and mention you need a fit note assessment when booking.
