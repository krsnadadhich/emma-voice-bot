Riverside Green Surgery — Repeat Prescriptions

If you take regular medication, you can request a repeat prescription in
one of three ways:
1. Through the NHS App, if you are registered for online services.
2. By handing in a signed repeat prescription slip at reception.
3. By emailing the practice using the address on your last prescription slip.

Please allow 2 full working days (48 hours) for repeat prescriptions to be
processed. We cannot guarantee same-day turnaround.

We do not currently accept repeat prescription requests over the phone,
as this creates a risk of prescribing errors.

Once ready, prescriptions can be collected from the surgery or sent
electronically to your nominated pharmacy, if you have one set up.
