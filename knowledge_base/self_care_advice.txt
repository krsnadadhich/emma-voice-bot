Riverside Green Surgery — Self-Care for Minor Ailments

Many common conditions can be safely managed at home without seeing a GP:

- Common cold or mild flu: rest, fluids, and over-the-counter paracetamol
  or ibuprofen. See a GP if symptoms last longer than 3 weeks.
- Mild diarrhoea or vomiting in adults: stay hydrated with water or oral
  rehydration sachets. See a GP if it lasts more than 2 days, or sooner
  for young children or elderly patients.
- Minor cuts and grazes: clean with water, cover with a plaster or
  dressing. Seek attention if the wound is deep, won't stop bleeding, or
  shows signs of infection (redness, swelling, warmth, pus).
- Mild hay fever: antihistamines from a pharmacy are usually effective.

Local pharmacies can also advise on and treat many minor ailments without
needing a GP appointment, often on a same-day, walk-in basis.

This guidance is general information only and is not a substitute for a
clinical assessment. If you are ever unsure or symptoms are severe or
worsening, contact the surgery or NHS 111.
